HBI Foundation

Ensuring equitable access, community engagement, and the long-term sustainability required to expand participation in innovation.

Access & Sustainability

Removing barriers and building durable pathways

The Foundation supports scholarships, mentorship, community programming, resource development, volunteer engagement, and partnerships that enable learners and communities to participate fully in the innovation economy.

Scholarships & Access

Reduce financial barriers to high-quality STEAM programs, technology experiences, and career exploration.

Mentorship

Connect learners with professionals, researchers, entrepreneurs, educators, and community leaders.

Community Engagement

Co-design relevant programs and solutions with schools, families, nonprofits, and neighborhood partners.

Volunteer Network

Mobilize technical experts, corporate teams, alumni, and community supporters.

Workforce Equity

Create pathways into high-demand technical fields for communities historically underrepresented in innovation.

Long-Term Sustainability

Build the funding, partnerships, infrastructure, and measurement systems required for lasting impact.
