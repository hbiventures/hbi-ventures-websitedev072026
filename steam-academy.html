ARTS, Digital Media & Storytelling

Where creativity meets technology

The NextGen Arts Cohort, launched in 2021, connected students with entertainment and creative-industry professionals while they developed digital storytelling, film production, screenwriting, editing, audio, collaboration, and presentation skills.

Students produced three original short films with support from a network that included Tyler Perry Studios, the Dallas Austin Foundation, the Georgia Governor’s Office of Film, Georgia Film Academy, Boston University Theatre Program, and the Urban League of Greater Atlanta.
