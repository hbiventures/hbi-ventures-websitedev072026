About HBI Ventures

Innovation, education, and community impact working as one system

HBI Ventures, LLC is an innovation and impact organization focused on building, testing, and scaling emerging technologies while developing the next generation of diverse technical talent.

Our model

Operating at the intersection of innovation, education, and community impact, HBI is built on three integrated pillars: HBI STEAM Academy, HBI Innovation Foundry, and HBI Foundation.

Together, these pillars create a continuous pipeline from education to innovation to workforce and community impact, enabling HBI Ventures to deliver globally informed, locally relevant, and measurable outcomes.

  • Develop diverse, future-ready technical talent
  • Apply emerging technologies to real challenges
  • Build and test products, programs, and solutions
  • Expand access to innovation pathways
  • Create measurable community and workforce outcomes
